GAME RULES

Alien Rush Bonanza · 6×5 Tumble Slot
How to Win
Win combinations are formed by 8 or more matching symbols appearing anywhere on the field (Gates of Olympus style — no paylines, position doesn't matter). Winning symbols explode and new ones tumble down for chained wins.

Special Symbols
Wild
The WILD substitutes for any paying symbol (premium or low) when forming winning combinations. It does not substitute for Scatter or Multiplier symbols.
Scatter & Free Spins
Up to 6 scatters can land per spin. The bonus is triggered with:
• 3 scatters → 10 free spins
• 4 scatters → 15 free spins
• 5 scatters → 20 free spins
• 6 scatters → 25 free spins

Retrigger during free spins (3+ scatters):
• 3 → +5 spins · 4 → +8 · 5 → +12 · 6 → +18
Multiplier Symbol (Bombs)
The multiplier bomb (×2 → ×50, plus rare ×100 and ultra-rare ×1000 in super bonus) lands as a regular grid symbol. Multipliers only apply if at least one bomb is on the field after all cascades complete.
• Base game: all on-grid bombs are summed and multiply that spin's win once.
• Bonus: every bomb is added to a persistent tracker. The tracker total multiplies every winning spin until the bonus ends (cap ×250).
• Super bonus: bombs fall constantly, plus rare ×100 and ultra-rare ×1000 bombs are in the pool.
Big Win Tiers
Wins are announced when they reach: BIG ×20, SUPER ×100, MEGA ×200, EPIC ×500, SENSATIONAL ×1000+ the bet.
