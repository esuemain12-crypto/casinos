How Tournaments Work
01
Auto Entry

No registration, no opt-in. The moment you place a qualifying wager in an active tournament game, you are automatically entered and your points start accumulating.

02
Earn Points

Standard scoring: 100 points per $100 wagered. Some events use multiplier scoring based on win ratios or special symbol triggers. Check each tournament for details.

03
Climb the Board

Live standings update every 60 seconds. Track your position in real time on the Leaderboard page. Points from multiple sessions in the same event are combined automatically.

04
Win Real Cash

Prizes are credited directly to your main balance within 60 minutes of tournament end. Cash prizes carry zero wagering requirements — withdraw immediately or keep playing.
