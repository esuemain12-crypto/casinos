How Points Are Earned

Point Accumulation

Every $100 wagered in real money earns 100 Tournament Points. Points accumulate throughout the competition period and are tracked in real time on this leaderboard.

All Games Count

Tournament points are earned across all games in the Zone 51 catalog. Slots, live casino and table games all contribute equally. Bonus wagering does not count toward tournament points.

Prize Payout

At the end of each tournament period, prizes are credited directly to winners' cash balances. No wagering requirements apply to tournament winnings. Monthly and All Time prizes are paid quarterly.

Tournament Terms

  • Weekly tournaments run from Monday 00:00 UTC to Sunday 23:59 UTC. Results are finalized within 1 hour of close.
  • Minimum qualifying wager to appear on the leaderboard: $500 in the tournament period.
  • In the event of a tie in points, the player who reached that points total first will be ranked higher.
  • Zone 51 reserves the right to disqualify any player found to be abusing bonuses or using restricted strategies.
  • Monthly and All Time leaderboards are updated every 24 hours. Prizes are distributed on the 1st of each month.
  • Players must have a verified account to receive tournament prizes. KYC verification must be complete before payout.
